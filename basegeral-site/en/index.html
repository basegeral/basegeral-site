Experience, precision and technical capability.

BaseGeral Modelagem e Corte provides specialised modelling and cutting services, helping companies in the footwear industry and related sectors turn their ideas into concrete solutions.

Focused on precision, quality and efficiency, we work with a range of materials and production needs, adapting each service to each client's requirements.

Our location in Felgueiras keeps us close to one of the main hubs of the footwear industry in Portugal.

Services

A specialised response for every stage of the modelling and cutting process.

Footwear Modelling

Development and preparation of models for production, with attention to precision and to each project's specific needs.

Footwear Cutting

Professional cutting of different materials for footwear production.

Leather Cutting

Cutting of natural leather and other materials used in the industry.

Lining Cutting

Preparation and cutting of materials used inside footwear.

Canvas & Fabric Cutting

Cutting of different fabrics and technical materials.

Heel Counter Cutting

Cutting of materials used in the structure and reinforcement of footwear.

Foam Cutting

Cutting of foams for different industrial applications.

Card Cutting

Cutting of card and similar materials for different production needs.

Sequin Application

Application of sequins and decorative elements onto materials for different applications.

Pattern Cutting

Cutting of patterns and components according to project specifications.

CAD Digitisation

CAD digitisation and preparation of models for integration into development and production processes.
